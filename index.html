ℹ️ About this prototype

A mobile-first, no-reload feel-test of KingsAge. Time runs at 60×. Build and recruit, scout a camp, raid it and watch the battle (retreat to save survivors). Build an Academy, train Noblemen, and send them on a winning attack to grind a camp's loyalty to zero — it then joins your realm as a second village. Tap your own villages on the map to reinforce them with troops from another village. Meanwhile Warlord Kaas raids your capital — scout, clear, and finally conquer his stronghold to end the raids and win. Tap the 🛠️ sandbox button (bottom-right) for God mode (∞ resources), 100× training/build speed, and instant tools to stress-test everything. Your game auto-saves on this device, so you can pick up where you left off.
